A chat assistant that answers quick questions about emergency contacts, pharmacy timings, shift schedules and bed availability at any hour.

System & Document Outline

Shift handovers are often written on paper or passed on verbally, so details about free beds, oxygen stock and patient billing can get lost. MediShift Ops moves that information into one shared screen.

  1. Register and sign in: staff create an account with their employee ID and hospital division, then log in securely.
  2. Review the dashboard: the incoming shift checks ward status, bed counts and urgent alerts in one view.
  3. Handover: the outgoing team confirms duty nurse and doctor assignments for each ward.
  4. Calculate and ask: use the calculator for supply and billing totals and the assistant for quick protocol answers.

UN SDG 3 (Good Health and Well-Being): faster, clearer handovers help patients get the right bed and care sooner.
UN SDG 9 (Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure): replaces paper registers with a simple, low-cost digital tool that any hospital with a web browser can use.
